## What
The Jobs page lets you edit a job's name and hourly rate, with an extra confirmation step whenever the rate itself changes.

## Why
A pay rate change affects payroll math for every future shift under that job, so it asks you to confirm before saving instead of applying it silently.

## Where
Sidebar > Jobs > Jobs table > click the edit action on a job's row.

## How
1. Open Jobs.
2. Click to edit the job you want to change.
3. Update the name and/or rate.
4. Click save - if the rate changed, you'll be asked to confirm.
5. Confirm to apply the new rate.

## What you'll see
Editing only the name (not the rate) saves immediately with no confirmation step - the confirmation only triggers when the numeric rate is different from what it was.
