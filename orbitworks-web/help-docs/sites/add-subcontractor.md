## What
The Jobs page lets you add a subcontractor - an outside company record with contact details, separate from job sites and jobs.

## Why
Subcontractor companies need their own record (contact name, email, phone, address) so they can be tracked and referenced elsewhere in the account without being treated as an employee or a location.

## Where
Sidebar > Jobs > "Subcontractors" section.

## How
1. Open Jobs.
2. Under Subcontractors, enter the company name, contact name, email, phone, and address (contact fields are optional).
3. Click Add subcontractor.
4. The subcontractor appears in the Subcontractors table, active by default.

## What you'll see
Only the company name is required - contact name, email, phone, and address can be filled in later by editing the row.
