## What
The Jobs page lets you add a job site - a physical location employees can be assigned to and clock in/out at.

## Why
Job sites are what tie an employee's clock events and a supervisor's assignments to a specific place. An employee must have at least one assigned site to be usable in Time Tracking.

## Where
Sidebar > Jobs > "Job Sites" section, at the top form.

## How
1. Open Jobs.
2. Under Job Sites, enter a site name (required) and address (optional).
3. Click Add site.
4. The new site appears in the Job Sites table below, active by default.

## What you'll see
Click a row's edit action to rename the site or change its address. Toggle Status to mark a site inactive when it's no longer in use - inactive sites stay in history but shouldn't be assigned to new employees.
