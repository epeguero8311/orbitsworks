## What
The Jobs page also manages "Jobs" - job titles paired with an hourly pay rate, separate from job sites (locations).

## Why
Jobs standardize what an employee is paid for a given role, so payroll math in Reports and Time Tracking pulls from one consistent rate instead of a rate typed per-employee.

## Where
Sidebar > Jobs > "Jobs" section (below Job Sites).

## How
1. Open Jobs.
2. Under Jobs, enter a job name and hourly rate.
3. Click Add job.
4. The job appears in the Jobs table, active by default.

## What you'll see
A job needs both a name and a valid numeric hourly rate - the form blocks submission with "Enter a job name and a valid hourly rate." if either is missing or the rate isn't a number.
