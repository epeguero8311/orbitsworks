## What
Job sites, jobs, and subcontractors on the Jobs page can be toggled between active and inactive - there's no delete, only a status toggle.

## Why
Deleting would break the history of past clock events, reports, and assignments that reference that record. Deactivating keeps all of that intact while hiding the record from new assignments going forward.

## Where
Sidebar > Jobs > the Status column in any of the three tables (Job Sites, Jobs, Subcontractors).

## How
1. Open Jobs.
2. Find the row for the site, job, or subcontractor.
3. Click its Status toggle to flip between active and inactive.

## What you'll see
Inactive records stay visible in their table (usually shown grayed out or labeled "Inactive") so you can reactivate them later - they aren't deleted or hidden from history.
