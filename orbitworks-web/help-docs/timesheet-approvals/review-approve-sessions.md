## What
The Timesheet Approvals page shows a row per completed clock-in/clock-out session (a "session"), viewable one day at a time or a full week at once, and lets you mark sessions pending or approved individually or in bulk.

## Why
Every paired clock-in/out becomes a session that needs review before it's treated as final - this is where an admin signs off on hours before they flow into payroll/reports.

## Where
Sidebar > Timesheet Approvals.

## How
1. Open Timesheet Approvals. It defaults to today's date in Day view.
2. Switch between Day and Week view using the toggle in the header.
3. Use the header to step forward/back a day (or week) or jump to a specific date.
4. Each row shows the employee, company (main or subcontractor), site, clock-in/out time, Shift (total time), Break, Worked (Shift minus Break), and status.
5. Click a row's status dropdown to change it between Pending and Approved, or check the boxes on multiple rows and click "Approve All" in the bar that appears.

## What you'll see
- The header shows a pending count so you can see at a glance how much is left to review.
- Only sessions with both a clock-in and a matching clock-out show up as a row - an employee still clocked in (no "out" yet) won't have a row until they clock out, though a "Clocked In" badge marks them elsewhere in the table.
- A row that resulted from a supervisor override on mobile shows an amber "Override" badge next to the employee's name - click it to see who authorized it, what action it was, and the reason they gave. Rows with an unreviewed override are highlighted.
- A row's pencil (edit) icon is disabled once it's Approved - set it back to Pending first to edit the underlying timestamps.
- You can't navigate earlier than the approvals cutover date (2026-09-06) - dates before that aren't tracked in this system.
