## What
The Timesheet Approvals page shows one day at a time, with a row per completed clock-in/clock-out session (a "session"), and lets you mark each session pending or approved.

## Why
Every paired clock-in/out becomes a session that needs review before it's treated as final - this is where an admin signs off on hours before they flow into payroll/reports.

## Where
Sidebar > Timesheet Approvals.

## How
1. Open Timesheet Approvals. It defaults to today's date.
2. Use the day header to step forward/back a day or jump to a specific date.
3. Each row shows the employee, company (main or subcontractor), site, clock-in/out time, hours, break time, and status.
4. Click a row's status control to toggle it between Pending and Approved.

## What you'll see
- The day header shows a pending count for that day so you can see at a glance how much is left to review.
- Only sessions with both a clock-in and a matching clock-out show up as a row - an employee still clocked in (no "out" yet) won't have a row until they clock out.
- You can't navigate earlier than the approvals cutover date (2026-09-06) - dates before that aren't tracked in this system.
