## What's different between Core and Pro
Core covers the basics well: clock in/out, timesheets, and approvals. Pro adds everything a growing crew needs to stop guessing and start proving - geolocation on every clock-in, job site cost analytics, temporary clock-in links for workers without the app, and a growing set of accountability tools like geofencing and face recognition.

## Why it's worth it
Core tells you WHEN someone clocked in. Pro tells you WHERE it happened and what it actually cost.

- Geolocation shows exactly where every clock-in and clock-out happened - on site, near site, or off site - with a map, address, and GPS accuracy on every event. No more "I was there, I swear" disputes.
- Job Site Cost Analytics turns raw hours into real numbers: labor cost per site, overtime by site, in-house vs. subcontractor spend, and which site is quietly costing more than it should - without opening a spreadsheet.
- Temp Clock-In Link lets a subcontractor or a one-day worker clock in on any phone with just a PIN and a photo - no app install, no onboarding delay, still fully logged and photo-verified.
- More is coming: geofencing (flagging or blocking clock-ins outside the job site) and face recognition build directly on top of what Pro already captures.

For any company running more than a couple of job sites, Pro usually pays for itself the first time it catches one avoidable overtime week or settles one "were they actually there" dispute.

## How to upgrade
Sidebar > Billing > toggle to "Pro" > choose a tier > confirm. It takes effect immediately and the rest of the current billing period is prorated.
