## What
Billing has a "Cancel subscription and return to the free plan" link that ends your paid subscription immediately and moves your company to the free plan, capped at 8 employees.

## Why
For when you no longer need a paid tier - it's a one-click way to drop back to the free plan rather than contacting support.

## Where
Sidebar > Billing > below "Current plan" (only shown if you're on a paid plan and not past due).

## How
1. Open Billing.
2. Click "Cancel subscription and return to the free plan."
3. Confirm twice - first a warning that you'll be capped at 8 employees, then a final "Are you sure?" step.
4. Your subscription cancels immediately.

## What you'll see
- Unlike downgrading to a specific paid tier, canceling does not let you pick who gets deactivated - if you're over the free plan's 8-employee cap, the system automatically deactivates the excess, non-supervisors first.
- The final confirmation shows exactly how many employees will be auto-deactivated before you confirm.
- This option is hidden if your account is past due - a past-due balance needs to be resolved first.
