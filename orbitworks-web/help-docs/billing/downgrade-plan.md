## What
Billing > Plans also handles downgrades - picking a lower-cap tier than your current plan, which may require deactivating employees first if you're over the new cap.

## Why
A plan's employee cap is enforced - if you have more active employees than the plan you're switching to allows, the system needs some deactivated before it can apply the new plan.

## Where
Sidebar > Billing > "Plans" section > click a lower tier's button.

## How
1. Open Billing.
2. Click the button on a tier below your current plan (it's labeled "Upgrade" regardless of direction).
3. Confirm twice, same as an upgrade.
4. If your active employee count is above the new plan's cap, a "Choose employees to deactivate" screen appears - select exactly the required number, then confirm.
5. The plan change is applied after the deactivations go through.

## What you'll see
On the deactivation screen you pick specific employees by name - it's manual, not automatic. Deactivated employees aren't deleted; you can reactivate anyone later from the Employees page.
