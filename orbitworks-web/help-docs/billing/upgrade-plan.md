## What
Billing > Plans lets you move to a plan with a higher employee cap - tiers run from 15 up to 100 employees, priced by cap.

## Why
Each plan caps how many total employees (including supervisors) you can have active. Upgrading raises that cap as you grow.

## Where
Sidebar > Billing > "Plans" section.

## How
1. Open Billing.
2. Find the tier you want and click its button (labeled "Upgrade" for any tier that isn't your current one).
3. Confirm twice - first a "Switch to [plan]?" step, then a final "Are you sure?" step warning your card will be charged or credited the prorated difference right away.
4. If payment needs to be collected (e.g. you're moving off the free plan), a checkout modal opens to complete it.

## What you'll see
Your current plan is marked "Current plan" and its button is disabled. Pricing is based on total employees including supervisors, shown as "[used] of [cap] employees used" under Current plan.
