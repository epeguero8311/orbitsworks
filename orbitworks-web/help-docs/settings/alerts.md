## What
Settings > Alerts controls which alerts show on the Overview dashboard, each with its own on/off toggle and a threshold: Max hours warning, Overtime warning, Missed clock out alert, and Max break time warning.

## Why
Lets you tune which attendance issues actually surface as alerts, and at what threshold, instead of getting notified about everything.

## Where
Sidebar > Settings > "Alerts" section.

## How
1. Open Settings.
2. Toggle each alert on/off and set its threshold:
   - Max hours warning - warn at N hours (default 8) worked in a day.
   - Overtime warning - warn at N hours/week (shares the weekly overtime threshold field).
   - Missed clock out alert - alert N minutes (default 30) after a shift should have ended.
   - Max break time warning - warn after N minutes (default 15) on break.
3. Click Save changes.

## What you'll see
Turning an alert off removes it from the Overview dashboard entirely, regardless of its threshold - the threshold field only matters while the toggle is on.
