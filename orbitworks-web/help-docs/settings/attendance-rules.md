## What
Settings > Time & Attendance Rules controls a grace period (minutes before a clock-in counts as late) plus three toggles: Allow early clock in, Allow late clock out, and Auto clock out.

## Why
These define what counts as "on time" and whether employees can clock in/out outside business hours, and whether the system automatically closes out a shift someone forgot to end.

## Where
Sidebar > Settings > "Time & Attendance Rules" section.

## How
1. Open Settings.
2. Set the Grace period in minutes - e.g. 5 minutes on an 8:00 start means 8:05 still counts as on time, 8:06 counts as late.
3. Toggle Allow early clock in / Allow late clock out to permit or block clocking in/out outside business hours.
4. Toggle Auto clock out to automatically clock out employees who forget to.
5. Click Save changes.

## What you'll see
These toggles are enforced today in the web Time Tracking manual-entry flow (early/late clock-in blocking, auto clock-out on dashboard load). Full enforcement in the mobile clock-in flow is still being built, so behavior there may lag what's set here.
