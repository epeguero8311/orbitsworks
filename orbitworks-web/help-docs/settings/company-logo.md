## What
Settings > Company profile lets you upload/change your company logo and edit your company name.

## Why
The logo shows up in the top bar of the dashboard - this is where you set it.

## Where
Sidebar > Settings > "Company profile" section (top of the page).

## How
1. Open Settings.
2. Click Upload logo (or Change logo if one's already set) and pick an image file.
3. Edit the Company name field if needed.
4. Click Save changes at the bottom of the page.

## What you'll see
The logo preview updates immediately when you pick a file, but it isn't actually saved to your account until you click Save changes at the bottom.
