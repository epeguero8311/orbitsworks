## What
Settings > App Settings has one toggle, "Allow supervisor override," which controls whether supervisors can override normal clock-in rules from the mobile app.

## Why
Gives you a company-wide switch to lock down or loosen how much flexibility supervisors have to clock employees in/out outside the normal rules on-site.

## Where
Sidebar > Settings > "App Settings" section.

## How
1. Open Settings.
2. Toggle Allow supervisor override on or off.
3. Click Save changes.

## What you'll see
This only affects the mobile app's clock-in flow - it has no effect on the web Time Tracking manual entry page, which is admin-only regardless of this setting.
