## What
Settings > Business hours sets a single company-wide open and close time.

## Why
Business hours are the baseline Time Tracking checks against for early clock-in and late clock-out rules, and the auto clock-out feature uses the close time to know when to close out a forgotten shift.

## Where
Sidebar > Settings > "Business hours" section.

## How
1. Open Settings.
2. Set the open time and close time.
3. Click Save changes.

## What you'll see
This applies company-wide for now - there's no per-site or per-employee override yet, so all job sites share the same business hours.
