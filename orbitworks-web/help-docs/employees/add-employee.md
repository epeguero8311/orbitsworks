## What
The Employees page lets you add a new employee record.

## Why
Employees need a record before they can be assigned to job sites or clocked in/out - unlike admins and supervisors, employees have no login of their own.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > the "Add employee" form at the top of the page.

## How
1. Open Employees.
2. Enter the employee's name (required).
3. Pick a job from the Jobs list (uses that job's pay rate), or enter a custom job title and hourly rate instead.
4. Optionally assign them to a subcontractor company instead of the main company.
5. Assign one or more job sites.
6. Optionally upload a reference photo (for face-match clock-in).
7. Save.

## What you'll see
- A unique backup clock-in PIN is generated automatically when the employee is created - it's shown once right after saving, and can be looked up again anytime from the employees table.
- If your company is at its plan's employee limit, adding is blocked and an upgrade prompt appears instead of an error.
