## What
OrbitsWorks has four levels of access: Owner, Admin, Supervisor, and Employee.

## Why
Different people on a team need different amounts of access - an office admin shouldn't need billing access just to review timesheets, and a foreman on-site only needs enough authority to fix a clock mistake, not the full dashboard.

## Where
Roles are granted from Sidebar > Employees, either through Admin/Supervisor invites or the access toggles on an individual employee's edit modal.

## How
- **Owner** - the account that created the company. Full dashboard access plus Billing, which no other role can see. There is exactly one owner per company.
- **Admin** - full web dashboard access (employees, sites, timesheets, reports, settings) except Billing. Has their own login.
- **Supervisor** - mobile app access to override clock-in rules and manage breaks on assigned job sites. Does not get the web dashboard. Can have a login (email invite) or be PIN-only (no login, authorizes with just their 4-digit PIN).
- **Employee** - clocks in/out (face-match or PIN) and has no dashboard or override authority. Has no login of their own.

A single person can hold more than one of these at once - an employee can be granted Supervisor access, Admin access, or both, independently.

## What you'll see
- Only the owner can see or reach the Billing page - everyone else is redirected if they try the URL directly.
- Admin and Supervisor are granted two different ways: invite a brand-new person by email (Sidebar > Employees > Admins/Supervisors sections), or grant access to an existing employee record from that employee's edit modal.
