## What
The Supervisors section at the bottom of the Employees page lets you invite someone by email to join as a supervisor, with a shareable signup link.

## Why
Supervisors need their own login (unlike regular employees), and get access to manage their assigned sites from the mobile app - this is how that access gets granted.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > "Supervisors" section (below the employees table).

## How
1. Open Employees and scroll to Supervisors.
2. Enter the supervisor's email.
3. Assign the job site(s) they'll manage.
4. Submit - this creates a pending invite.
5. Copy the invite link and send it to them, or they can use the emailed invite.
6. Once accepted, they show up in the employees list too, with a Supervisor badge.

## What you'll see
- You can't send a second pending invite to the same email - it's blocked with "There's already a pending invite for that email."
- Pending invites can be canceled with a confirmation step before the supervisor accepts.
