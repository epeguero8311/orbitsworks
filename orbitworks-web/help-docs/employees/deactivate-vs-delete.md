## What
Employees can be turned off two different ways: a reversible Deactivate/Reactivate toggle in the employees table, and a permanent Delete option inside the employee edit modal.

## Why
Deactivating is the normal way to handle someone who's no longer working (keeps all their history and can be undone); deleting is a hard, permanent removal for records that shouldn't exist at all.

## Where
- Deactivate/Reactivate: Sidebar > Employees > the status toggle on the employee's row in the table.
- Delete: Sidebar > Employees > click the employee's row > Delete employee button inside the modal.

## How
**Deactivate/Reactivate:**
1. Open Employees.
2. Click the Deactivate (or Reactivate) action on the employee's row.

**Delete:**
1. Open Employees and click the employee's row.
2. Click "Delete employee."
3. Confirm "Delete [name]? This can't be undone."

## What you'll see
Deactivating keeps the employee in the table (sorted to the bottom, marked Inactive) and preserves all their clock history - they can be reactivated anytime. Deleting removes the employee record entirely and cannot be undone, so it's meant for records added in error, not for normal offboarding.
