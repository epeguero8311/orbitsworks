## What
The Admins section at the bottom of the Employees page lets you invite someone by email to join as an admin, with a shareable signup link.

## Why
An admin gets full web dashboard access - employees, sites, timesheets, reports, and settings - without needing to be the account owner or share the owner's login. Billing is the only thing admins can't see.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > "Admins" section (below the Supervisors section).

## How
1. Open Employees and scroll to Admins.
2. Enter the admin's email.
3. Submit - this creates a pending invite.
4. Copy the invite link and send it to them, or they can use the emailed invite.
5. Once accepted, they show up in the employees list too, with an Admin badge.

## What you'll see
- You can't send a second pending invite to the same email - it's blocked with "There's already a pending invite for that email," and this check also blocks against a pending Supervisor invite for the same address.
- A pending invite's email delivery status shows as Sending, Sent, or Failed - if it's stuck or failed, use Resend rather than creating a duplicate invite.
- Pending invites can be canceled with a confirmation step before the admin accepts.
- Unlike Supervisor invites, Admin invites don't ask for job site assignments - admins see every site by default.

## Turning an existing employee into an admin
There's no separate "convert" flow for admins the way there's no separate one for supervisors either. If the person already has a regular employee record and hasn't been linked to a login yet, open their record from the Employees table and grant Supervisor access with an email - once they accept, their record becomes linked and an Admin access toggle appears alongside Supervisor access, which you can then turn on. See "Editing Employee Details" for the linked-employee access toggles.
