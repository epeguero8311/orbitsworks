## What
The employee edit modal has a "Company" field to reassign which company (your main company, or one of your subcontractors) an employee currently works under.

## Why
An employee's company can change over time - this lets you reflect that without losing their history.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > click the employee's row > "Company" section.

## How
1. Open Employees and click the employee.
2. In the Company section, select the main company or a subcontractor from the dropdown.
3. Provide a reason for the change if prompted.
4. Save.

## What you'll see
This only affects clock events going forward - past clock events stay attributed to whichever company was in effect when they were recorded, so historical reports won't change.
