## What
Clicking an employee's row in the Employees table opens a modal to edit their name, job/pay rate, phone, date of birth, assigned job sites, reference photo, and whether they're a supervisor.

## Why
Employee details change over time - job changes, new sites, updated contact info - and this is the one place to update all of it.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > click any row in the employees table.

## How
1. Open Employees and click the employee's row.
2. Update whichever fields need changing (name, job/rate, phone, date of birth, assigned sites, photo).
3. Toggle "Supervisor" on/off if their role is changing.
4. Save.

## What you'll see
Toggling Supervisor on doesn't create a login by itself - that still goes through Supervisor Invites. This toggle just marks the existing employee record as supervisor-flagged.
