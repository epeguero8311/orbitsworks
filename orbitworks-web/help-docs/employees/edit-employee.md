## What
Clicking an employee's row in the Employees table opens a modal to edit their name, job/pay rate, phone, date of birth, assigned job sites, reference photo, PIN, and Supervisor/Admin access.

## Why
Employee details change over time - job changes, new sites, updated contact info - and this is the one place to update all of it.

## Where
Sidebar > Employees > click any row in the employees table.

## How
1. Open Employees and click the employee's row.
2. Update whichever fields need changing (name, job/rate, phone, date of birth, assigned sites, photo).
3. Adjust Supervisor access and/or Admin access if their role is changing - see "Granting or Removing Supervisor and Admin Access for an Employee" for how those toggles behave.
4. Save.

## What you'll see
The PIN field near the top can be edited directly, and is checked for duplicates before anything else in the form saves.
