## What
Pro plan companies get an Analytics page that turns clocked hours into real labor cost numbers, broken down by job site.

## Why
Lets admins and owners see where labor money is actually going - which site costs the most, how much of that is overtime, and how in-house cost compares to subcontractor cost - without exporting anything to a spreadsheet.

## Where
Sidebar > Analytics.

## How
1. Open Analytics.
2. Pick a date range - This week, Last week, This month, Last month, or a custom range.
3. Optionally filter to one job site, and to in-house workers only, subcontractors only, or both.
4. Click any site in the results to open its drilldown: that site's employee-by-employee cost and its weekly cost trend.

## What you'll see
- Total labor cost, total hours, total overtime cost, and the single most expensive site for the selected range.
- A cost-per-site breakdown and an in-house vs. subcontractor cost split.
- A weekly cost trend chart.
- A comparison against the previous equivalent period (e.g. this month vs. last month) so you can see whether cost is trending up or down.
- If you're on Core, an upgrade prompt shows in place of the page instead.
