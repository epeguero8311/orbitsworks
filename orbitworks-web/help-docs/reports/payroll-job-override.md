## What
The Daily breakdown section of Reports (below the Payroll table) lets you override which job/pay rate applies to an employee for a single day, for payroll calculation purposes only.

## Why
Sometimes an employee worked under a different job/rate for one day than what's on their record - this recalculates that day's pay in the report without changing their actual clock session or their permanent job assignment.

## Where
Sidebar > Reports > "Daily breakdown" section > use the arrows to step to the day in question, then change the job dropdown on the employee's row.

## How
1. Open Reports and run a report for your date range.
2. Scroll to Daily breakdown and navigate to the specific day.
3. Pick a different job from the dropdown on the employee's row.
4. The row's pay recalculates immediately using that job's hourly rate.

## What you'll see
This override is report-time-only - it never touches the underlying clock events or the employee's real job assignment, and it resets if you change the report's date range or reload the page.
