## What
The Time Trends section of Reports shows two charts - average hours worked per week, and employees clocked in per day - plus an "Avg hours / employee" stat for the selected range.

## Why
Surfaces staffing and workload patterns over time (busy weeks, thin days) that a single table of numbers wouldn't make obvious at a glance.

## Where
Sidebar > Reports > "Time Trends" section.

## How
Nothing to configure directly - both charts and the stat recalculate whenever you change the date range and click Refresh on the Reports page.

## What you'll see
Both charts are built from the same clock event data as the rest of the report, so they'll always match the totals shown elsewhere on the page for the same date range.
