## What
Reports can be exported two ways: an "Export" dropdown at the top that downloads everything for the date range at once, and a separate export menu lower on the page for exporting one report type at a time (Payroll, Timesheets, Employees, or Attendance) as Excel or CSV.

## Why
Sometimes you want one combined file for the whole range (payroll run, audit), other times just one slice (e.g. a CSV of timesheets to import elsewhere).

## Where
Sidebar > Reports. "Export" dropdown is top-right, next to the page title. The per-type export menu sits further down the page, above the report sections.

## How
1. Open Reports and set your date range.
2. For everything at once: click Export (top right) > choose Excel or CSV.
3. For one report type: use the export menu lower on the page, pick Payroll / Timesheets / Employees / Attendance, then Excel or CSV.

## What you'll see
The Export button only appears once a report has finished calculating for the current date range - it's hidden while loading or before you've run a report.

Any sessions still pending approval on the Timesheet Approvals page (for dates on/after the approvals cutover date) are excluded from every export here - Payroll, Timesheets, Employees, and Attendance - until approved. Their hours won't appear in totals at all.
