## What
The Shift Notes section of Reports lists notes supervisors left during the selected date range, with the date, site, note text, and who wrote it.

## Why
Gives admins visibility into on-site context supervisors flagged - incidents, callouts, anything worth a written note - without needing to ask around.

## Where
Sidebar > Reports > "Shift Notes" section, near the bottom of the report results.

## How
Nothing to configure - the table just reflects whatever notes fall inside your selected date range. Change the range and click Refresh to see a different window.

## What you'll see
If no notes were left in the selected range, it shows "No shift notes for this date range." instead of an empty table.
