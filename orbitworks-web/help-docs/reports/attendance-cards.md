## What
The Attendance section of Reports shows four stat cards for the selected date range: On-time %, Late %, Avg. arrival time, and Avg. departure time.

## Why
A quick, high-level read on punctuality across the company for the range, without digging into individual clock events.

## Where
Sidebar > Reports > "Attendance" section (near the top of the report results).

## How
These cards update automatically whenever you change the date range and click Refresh - there's nothing to configure on the cards themselves.

## What you'll see
Each card shows "-" instead of a number if there isn't enough attendance data in the selected range to calculate it.
