## What
Pro plan companies automatically capture GPS coordinates for every clock-in and clock-out from the mobile app, a Temp Clock-In Link, and supervisor overrides. It does not apply to admin manual entries from the web dashboard, since the admin isn't at the site.

## Why
Lets admins, owners, and supervisors confirm where a clock-in or clock-out actually happened, not just when - and see at a glance whether it was at the job site, nearby, or somewhere else entirely.

## Where
Time Tracking > click any clock event > the Clock in / Clock out card in the detail popup.

## How
1. Nothing to turn on - Pro companies capture location automatically starting from the next clock-in.
2. Open Time Tracking and click a clock event to open its detail popup.
3. Each card (Clock in and Clock out) shows its own location, if it has one.

## What you'll see
- A small map with a pin at the exact point.
- The nearest street address, or raw coordinates if the address couldn't be resolved.
- A colored badge comparing the point to the event's job site: green "On site", amber "Near site", red "Off site", or gray "No site to compare" if the event has no job site or that job site doesn't have a location set yet.
- A GPS accuracy label (High, Medium, or Low) - if the accuracy circle is too loose to trust against a small job site, that's called out too.
- "Location not shared" if the employee's device denied location permission. The clock-in or clock-out still goes through either way - location is never required and never blocks it.
- Nothing shown at all for clock events from before this feature existed, or for admin manual entries - those never carry location data.
