## What
A Missed Clock-Out alert fires when a shift opened on a prior calendar day and never got a matching Clock Out.

## Why
Prevents open sessions from silently inflating hours or breaking payroll reports.

## Where
Dashboard Overview > Alerts panel.

## How
From the alert, you can Clock Out now, Edit Time to set the correct out-time, or Ignore it (ignoring only suppresses that specific date's instance, not future recurrences).

## What you'll see
If Auto Clock Out is enabled in Settings, most of these resolve automatically at business close and this alert will rarely appear. See "Auto Clock Out" for how that works.
