## What
The Time Tracking page lets an admin or supervisor manually clock an employee in or out.

## Why
Used when an employee cannot use face-match clock-in (device issue, forgot photo, or you are backfilling a missed punch).

## Where
Sidebar > Time Tracking.

## How
1. Open Time Tracking.
2. Optionally pick a job site to narrow the employee list (not required).
3. Find the employee, choose Clock In or Clock Out.
4. Add an optional note.
5. Confirm. The event appears immediately in the Recent Events log below.

## What you'll see
Each event shows a source badge: Face Match, Supervisor Override, or Admin Manual, so you can tell how the punch was created.
