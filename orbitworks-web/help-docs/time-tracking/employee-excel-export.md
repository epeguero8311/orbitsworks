## What
From the Time Tracking lookup tool, you can export a single employee's clock history to an Excel file, pairing their clock-ins/outs into shifts with hours and pay.

## Why
Gives you a shareable, printable record for one employee (for payroll, disputes, or recordkeeping) without exporting the whole company's report.

## Where
Sidebar > Time Tracking > lookup section > search by employee, then use the Excel export action.

## How
1. Open Time Tracking and run a lookup with an employee selected (from-date/to-date optional).
2. Trigger the Excel export.
3. The file downloads with that employee's paired shifts, daily/weekly totals, and pay based on their hourly rate and the company's weekly overtime threshold.

## What you'll see
- An employee must be selected in the lookup filters - the export button does nothing without one.
- If no date range is set, it defaults to everything from 2000-01-01 through today.
- If the export fails to build (e.g. missing data), you'll see "Couldn't build this employee's timesheet. Try again."
